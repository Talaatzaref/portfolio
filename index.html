01 — ABOUT

Why I chose software.

What attracted me to programming wasn't just writing code. It was the feeling of taking a problem that looks complicated and breaking it down until the logic starts to make sense.

As I learned C++ and programming fundamentals, I found that I genuinely enjoyed the problem-solving side of development. I liked asking: “What should happen here?”, “How should the data be organized?” and “How can I make this solution cleaner?”

That naturally pushed my interest toward backend development and databases. With .NET, I found a space where I could combine programming logic, APIs and SQL Server to build the part of an application that makes everything work together.

I'm still early in my career, so I'm not going to pretend I've seen everything. What I do have is curiosity, persistence, and a habit of learning by building — then improving what I build.
